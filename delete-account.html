Delete Account – FreeTravel App

This page explains how users of the FreeTravel App can request deletion of their account and associated data.

How to request account deletion

You can delete your account directly from the app:

  1. Open the FreeTravel App
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Select Delete Account
  4. Confirm the deletion request

Alternatively, you can request account deletion by contacting us at:
Email: support@freetravelapp.com

Data that will be deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data will be permanently deleted:

  • User profile information
  • Email address
  • Username
  • Profile photo, if uploaded
  • Created routes and travel requests
  • Chat messages
  • Ratings and comments associated with the account

Data retention

Account deletion requests are processed within 30 days. After this period, the account and associated personal data will be permanently deleted.

Some limited data may be retained only if required by law, security, fraud prevention, or dispute resolution purposes.

Contact
